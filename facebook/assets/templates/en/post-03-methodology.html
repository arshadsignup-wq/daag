How an entry enters the archive
01A contributor submits a publicly visible comment with its source link.
02A moderator independently opens that link and confirms the comment is real, public and correctly attributed.
03It is categorised and tagged by incident type.
04It is published with context, a version number and an edit history.
05Anyone named may file a dispute, reviewed by a separate panel.
What we reject
Private messages · screenshots without a verifiable source · satire · quotes stripped of context
