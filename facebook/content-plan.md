# দাগ · Facebook Content Plan

The Page is a **funnel to the archive, not a mirror of it.** Names, screenshots and
profile links live on daag-bd.com, where the methodology, version history and appeals
process frame them. On Facebook they get the Page taken down.

---

## 0. The rule that protects everything else

**Never post on the Page:**

- Screenshots of comments, even with the name blurred, reverse-crop and context make
  people identifiable, and blurring is read as an admission of intent by reviewers
- Names, usernames, profile links or workplaces of anyone in the archive
- Anything that reads as a call to action against a person ("report him", "share widely
  so he's recognised", "let his employer know")
- Unverified incidents or breaking cases before moderation has cleared them
- Names, photos or identifying details of anyone targeted, even from already-public coverage

**Always post instead:** aggregate patterns, redacted and translated quotes, methodology,
explainers, resources, submission calls. Every card ends at the same place: `daag-bd.com`.

This is not only Meta policy risk. It is the exact line in the project documentation
between an archive and a shaming tool, holding it on the loudest surface is what makes
the claim credible.

## 1. Content pillars

| # | Pillar | Share | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pattern analytics**: aggregate findings from the archive | 30% | `template-stat.png` |
| 2 | **Redacted entries**: one quote, no identity | 25% | `post-05-quote.png` |
| 3 | **Explainers**: what online abuse and victim-blaming look like | 20% | Text or simple card |
| 4 | **Methodology & transparency**: how verification and appeals work | 15% | Text + link |
| 5 | **Resources & submission calls**: helplines, how to contribute | 10% | Text + link |

**Cadence:** 3 posts a week, Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday, ~8-9pm Bangladesh time.
Do not post daily at launch; a thin Page posting constantly reads as a campaign.

**Caption formula:** Bangla throughout, link, then 3-4 hashtags. Mix Bangla and Latin
hashtags (#দাগ #Daag), people search both.

**Hashtags:** `#Daag #দাগ #StopVictimBlaming #GenderViolenceBD`
Rotate in: `#অনলাইনহেনস্তা #OnlineAbuse #সাইবারবুলিং`. Four maximum.

## 3. After the launch eight

The eight launch posts cover the project's whole argument. From week three you are
repeating those pillars with fresh material, not writing new arguments:

**Pattern analytics** is the engine. Use `template-stat.png`, drop in a real figure, and
always name the sample size and date range in the caption. A percentage without a
denominator is what gets a civic archive discredited, and it is the first thing a hostile
commenter will ask for.

**Redacted entries** run on `post-05-quote.png`. One archived comment, translated if it
came in English, no name, no screenshot. The standing disclaimer stays on every one:

```
পরিচয় এখানে দেওয়া হয়নি। যাচাই করা এন্ট্রি পুরো প্রসঙ্গ আর জবাব দেওয়ার সুযোগসহ
প্রকাশ করা হয় www.daag-bd.com-এ।
```

**Helplines** (post 8) go back up once a month. Confirm the numbers are still current
each time before posting.

**Methodology and appeals** (posts 3 and 7) get re-posted whenever a wave of criticism
arrives. They are the answer to almost every hostile comment, and re-posting beats
arguing in a thread.

## 4. Growth, without paying Meta

- **Instagram first, as a mirror.** Same cards, same schedule. Different reporting
  dynamics and a younger Dhaka audience. If the Page is restricted, the audience survives.
- **Email capture on the site is the real asset.** Every post drives to daag-bd.com;
  the site should ask for an email. A Page can vanish overnight. A list cannot.
- **Hand the methodology to journalists before the archive is big.** Rights
  organisations and tech or crime reporters at the dailies are the audience that gives
  Daag legitimacy and makes a takedown costly.
- **Never comment from the Page on a live news post.** It converts a documentation
  project into a participant in the fight, and it is the fastest route to a report wave.

---

## 5. Pre-post checklist

Before anything goes up:

- [ ] No name, handle, profile link or workplace anywhere in image or caption
- [ ] No screenshot of a comment
- [ ] Any statistic carries its sample size and date range
- [ ] Any claim about an incident is already verified in the archive
- [ ] Caption ends at `www.daag-bd.com`
- [ ] Bangla read aloud once before posting, if a sentence is hard to say, rewrite it.
      Stiff or translated-sounding Bangla gets noticed immediately and costs credibility
- [ ] Someone is available for the next 2 hours to moderate comments
