00

The voice these follow

Drawn from two places: the eight approved captions in copy-paste.html, and how Prothom Alo, Bangla Tribune and bdnews24 actually write. The newspaper conventions matter because they are already the conventions Daag lives by. Bangla journalism has solved the problem of writing about an accused person without degrading them, and that is precisely the line this project walks.

Register
Written how a thoughtful person actually posts, not how a paper prints. The first draft of this deck leaned on newspaper construction (দেওয়া হয়, করা হয়, প্রকাশ করা হয়) and read like a government circular. Passives are now active, and bureaucratic nouns (প্রক্রিয়া, সংস্করণ-ইতিহাস, কার্যদিবস) are gone or plainer.
Pronouns
Most lines speak generally (যে লিখেছে, তার হয়তো মনেও নেই) and the generic takes the plain form. Stacking যাঁকে / তাঁর / যিনি into every sentence is exactly what made it sound robotic. Dignity is carried by what the sentence says, not by decorating every pronoun.
Naming
Bangla papers name the place and withhold the person: কুড়িগ্রামে তরুণীকে ধর্ষণের অভিযোগ. People get identified by circumstance, not name. That convention stays; it is the same redaction logic already drawn into the films.
Allegation
অভিযোগ and অভিযুক্ত stay scrupulous. No caption states a comment's author as fact.
Rhythm
Short lines, frequent breaks, fragments where a fragment lands harder. Rhetorical questions are allowed and used (আর সপ্তাহখানেক পর?, তথ্যটাই ভুল?) because that is how people write when they mean it.
Punctuation
No emoji. No exclamation marks; the subject does not need amplifying. Quotes in “ ”, proper ।, Bangla numerals with লাখ / হাজার.
Shape
Opening statement → the turn → the guardrail (what Daag is not) where the film needs it → three or four hashtags. No URL: see below.
01

Before you post any of these

The first line is the whole caption on mobile. Facebook truncates a reel caption to roughly one line before আরও দেখুন. Every opening line below is written to stand alone if nobody taps. Do not add a greeting or a hook above it.

content-plan.md §0 governs these exactly as it governs the images: no name, handle, profile link or workplace in the caption, no screenshots of comments, nothing that reads as a call to action against a person. Moderate comments for two hours after each post; if nobody is free to do that, do not post. Cadence stays three a week: Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday, around 8–9pm Bangladesh time.

No link in any caption. A caption with an external URL in it gets throttled on Facebook, and on Instagram it is not tappable at all, so the address lives in three better places instead: burnt into the last three seconds of every film, on the Page's CTA button, and in the first comment. Post the reel, then paste the matching first comment below straight away.

Two first comments, one per kind of ask. Post it yourself from the Page within a minute of publishing, before anyone else comments, so it stays at the top and it is where লিংক কমেন্টে is pointing.
